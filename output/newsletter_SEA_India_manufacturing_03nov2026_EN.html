Core Component Supply Chain Update

Factories and facilities physically located in SEA or India

PCB / FPC

Meiko breaks ground on $500M circuit-board factory in Vietnam

Japan's Meiko Electronics has broken ground on a $500 million electronic circuit factory in Vietnam, extending a Vietnamese footprint that already includes five PCB manufacturing and assembly facilities and roughly $200 million committed at a Phu Tho plant. A further $50 million PCB plant is planned, with total planned Vietnamese investment approaching $1 billion.

📍 Vietnam · Groundbreaking

Vietnam's PCB cluster is moving from satellite capacity to a primary Japanese-owned supply node for ASEAN-localised customers.

Oct 2026 · Vietnam Investment Review

Sihui Fuji's Thai phase-two building due for completion by end-November 2026

First Quality Circuit (Sihui Fuji Electronics) is completing the phase-two building at its Thai PCB site at the Chonburi–Rayong junction, with investment for that phase expected to be finished by the end of November 2026. The plant is scheduled to begin production in Q2 2027 and will target communications, power-supply and satellite customers.

📍 Rayong/Chonburi, Thailand · Fit-out

Thailand's PCB build-out is entering the commissioning phase; qualification slots for 2027 programmes should be booked now.

Oct 2026 · mpbxchange

Compressors / Motors

Nidec's Embraco compressor plant in Maharashtra advances toward Q4 2026 ramp

Nidec Global Appliance is building a highly verticalised Embraco compressor hub at Chhatrapati Sambhaji Nagar (Aurangabad), Maharashtra, with production expected to begin in Q4 2026 and sales in early 2027. The $120 million-plus facility is designed for 6 million compressors a year and around 1,500 direct jobs, and will house its own R&D team.

📍 Maharashtra, India · Construction / ramp-up

Local compressor capacity removes a key import dependency for Indian refrigerator and AC OEMs and shortens the refrigeration supply chain.

Policy Focus

Incentives, approvals and regulatory gates affecting consumer-electronics manufacturing

🇮🇳 India — Dixon–Vivo JV cleared and moving to revenue

The 51:49 Dixon Technologies–Vivo Mobile India joint venture, approved under Press Note 3 in July 2026, is expected to close within two months, with revenue contributions beginning in the October–December quarter. JPMorgan estimates the JV adds 11 million handset volumes in FY27 and 22 million in FY28, with 12–15 million smartphones manufactured in FY27 against Vivo's ~35 million annual Indian sales.

Status: Approved · Commercial ramp
⚠️ Press Note 3 structuring remains the gate for any China-linked JV; expect similar 51:49 templates for future approvals.

🇮🇳 India — EPACK seeks extension of the white-goods PLI scheme

EPACK Durable is lobbying for an extension of the PLI scheme for white goods, citing revenue challenges, even as it executes a ₹1,084 crore Andhra Pradesh-backed expansion at Sri City covering room ACs, components, washing machines and televisions.

Indonesia

TKDN · Manufacturing
REPT

REPT BATTERO debuts first overseas manufacturing base in Indonesia

REPT BATTERO has announced the debut of its first overseas manufacturing base in Indonesia, with plans to obtain TKDN local-content certification and SNI Indonesian National Standard certification in June. The base is positioned to support Indonesia's energy-mix upgrade and green transition.

The move makes REPT one of the first Chinese battery-storage players to localise cell and pack production in Indonesia rather than shipping finished systems in, aligning with the country's local-content regime for downstream industrial buyers.

Compliance Alert

TKDN and SNI certification are the gating items — buyers should confirm certification status before committing to local-content claims in tenders.

Vietnam

Manufacturing & Supply Chain · OEM/EMS
Foxconn

Foxconn adds $287M in Bac Ninh and secures $551M of Quang Ninh licences

Foxconn has pumped an additional $287 million into its Bac Ninh facility as of 28 October 2026. Separately, the company has been granted licences for two projects in Quang Ninh province: a $264 million initiative for smart entertainment devices and a $287.2 million project on 12.4 hectares for smart-system equipment.

The combined $551 million in new licences is part of Foxconn's broader strategy to diversify its manufacturing footprint beyond China, deepening northern Vietnam's role as its principal non-China assembly base.

Strategic Read

Northern Vietnam's industrial-park supply is tightening around a handful of mega-tenants; component suppliers wanting co-location should secure land in Bac Ninh and Quang Ninh now.

28 Oct 2026 · Vietnam Investment Review · VnExpress International
Foxconn

Foxconn to invest $80M in Bac Giang for chip manufacturing expansion

Foxconn is set to invest $80 million in Bac Giang province to expand its chip manufacturing operations, adding to its northern Vietnam cluster alongside Bac Ninh and Quang Ninh. The province has become a secondary node in Foxconn's Vietnamese build-out.

The investment extends Foxconn's Vietnamese footprint beyond assembly into upstream electronics production, reinforcing the country's position in the company's China-plus-one network.

Sourcing Implication

Bac Giang is emerging as a viable secondary location for suppliers priced out of Bac Ninh — worth adding to site-selection shortlists.

02 Nov 2026 · Việt Nam News
Luxshare

Luxshare-ICT to add 9M VR units a year in Bac Ninh under $520M project

Luxshare-ICT Van Trung will add a new virtual-reality goggle line with a design capacity of 9 million products annually, lifting total annual capacity across its Bac Ninh factories from 257 million to 311 million items. Total investment capital for the project is $520 million.

The expansion concentrates Luxshare's VR and wearables ramp in northern Vietnam, deepening the country's role in Apple-adjacent and metaverse-device assembly.

Market Signal

VR headset supply is quietly re-basing to Vietnam; optics, display and precision-mechanics suppliers should follow their anchor customer's footprint.

Oct 2026 · The Investor
Goertek

Goertek pours $390M into Vietnam expansion with new product lines

Goertek is committing $390 million to Vietnamese expansion, with planned output including 1 million smart home security devices such as fingerprint-based smart locks, 2 million gaming consoles and 5 million additional units across other lines. A separate $20 million injection has also been made into its northern Vietnam plant.

The filing also flags unmanned aerial vehicle plans, with certain drone models currently at 45,000 units per year and scope for further scale-up.

Brand Watch

Goertek is diversifying beyond audio into security, gaming and drones in Vietnam — a signal that contract manufacturers are hedging single-category exposure.

India

Manufacturing · PLI & Policy · Supply Chain · OEM/EMS
Haier

Haier India commits ₹3,500 crore to a third plant over three to four years

Haier Appliances India plans to invest ₹3,500 crore over the next three to four years to expand its manufacturing footprint, including a third plant in either western or southern India. The location is still being finalised, with the plant expected to become operational between 2026 and 2027.

The investment will be rolled out in phases and is expected to generate around 12,000 direct and indirect jobs. The new plant will serve both domestic and export markets, aimed at lifting Haier's share in air conditioners, LED TVs and washing machines; Haier currently runs plants at Greater Noida and Pune and is targeting $2 billion revenue by end-2027.

Strategic Read

A third Haier plant plus the Greater Noida AC and injection-moulding expansion signals a full localisation push — component suppliers should expect local-content requirements to harden.

01 Nov 2026 · ET Retail · Indian Startup News
Foxconn

Foxconn's $1.5B Tamil Nadu display-module unit to create ~14,000 jobs

Foxconn is set to invest $1.5 billion to establish a smartphone display-module assembly unit in Tamil Nadu near Chennai, its first display-module unit in India. The company has secured a 500,000-square-foot site in Oragadam, and the Tamil Nadu government has approved the project.

The unit will primarily serve Apple, with additional capacity available for other manufacturers. A London Stock Exchange filing confirmed Foxconn's India unit, Yuzhan Technology India, will receive the investment.

Sourcing Implication

Display-module localisation closes one of the last import gaps in India's iPhone bill of materials — a meaningful step toward full-stack domestic assembly.

Oct 2026 · Supply Chain 24/7
Apple

India to assemble 28% of global iPhones in 2026; exports up 47%

India will assemble 28% of global iPhone shipments in 2026, up from 23% in 2025, with Apple exporting more than $13.2 billion worth of iPhones from India between April and August 2026 — a 47% increase year on year. Local production has scaled through Foxconn and Tata Electronics.

Exports to Europe and the US remain strong across several iPhone models shipped from India, reflecting a manufacturing base that has become more robust, scalable and export-oriented.

Strategic Read

India's share gain is now structural rather than opportunistic; component suppliers should plan Indian capacity against a 30%+ share trajectory.

Oct 2026 · Electronics Manufacturing News
Tata

Tata Electronics workforce hits 75,000, overtaking Foxconn in India headcount

Tata Electronics has expanded its workforce to 75,000 from 15,000, surpassing Foxconn's India headcount and making it Apple's largest contract manufacturer in the country by workforce. The 500-acre Hosur campus in Tamil Nadu is the core hub of iPhone assembly in India.

Growth has been supported by major capital investment, factory acquisitions — including former Wistron and Pegatron facilities — and rapid capacity buildout, with the company moving upstream into semiconductor assembly and test and component localisation.

Brand Watch

Tata is now the anchor tenant of India's Apple supply chain; suppliers should treat Hosur as a primary qualification target rather than a secondary site.

Oct 2026 · The Economic Times
Blue Star

Blue Star to invest ₹400 crore in FY26 AC and refrigeration capacity

Blue Star plans to invest ₹400 crore in fiscal 2026 to expand manufacturing capacity in room air conditioners and commercial refrigeration. The company is targeting expansion of AC production to 1.8 million units and a 14.3% market share by FY26.

The expansion will be taken up gradually across its manufacturing facilities, reflecting sustained domestic AC demand growth.

Market Signal

Indian AC capacity additions are running ahead of demand forecasts — component suppliers should watch for utilisation pressure in FY27.

Oct 2026 · NDTV Profit
Amber

Amber to start making Oppo, OnePlus and Realme phones from March 2027

Amber Enterprises is set to begin manufacturing Oppo, OnePlus and Realme smartphones from March 2027, with plans to double output in the second year of operations. The ramp adds a new contract-manufacturing entrant to India's handset assembly base.

The move comes as India's manufacturing sector reaches an inflection point after years of policy push, infrastructure investment and growing global interest.

Strategic Read

Amber's entry broadens the non-Dixon EMS bench in India — useful for brands seeking a second source without a China-linked JV structure.

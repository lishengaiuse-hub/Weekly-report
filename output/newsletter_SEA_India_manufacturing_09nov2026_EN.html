Global Industry Shifts

Cross-border moves with named SEA/India impact

Unimicron to close German PCB plant, redirect $1.6bn into Asian expansion

Unimicron plans to discontinue PCB manufacturing at its Geldern, Germany facility during H1 2027 while investing $1.6 billion in Asian expansion, spanning Taiwan, mainland China and Southeast Asia.

→ Downstream SEA impact: the redirected capex reinforces the region's role as the growth leg for advanced substrate and PCB capacity as European output is retired.
06 Nov 2026 · Printed Circuit Design & Fab

Daikin commits ¥100bn to Southeast Asia and India over four years

Daikin Industries will invest 100 billion yen ($711 million) across Southeast Asia and India over the next four years to build core parts and smaller air-conditioner production, including compact AC output in Indonesia.

→ Downstream SEA impact: localises compressor and core-part supply for ASEAN AC assembly and reduces import dependence for regional OEMs.
05 Nov 2026 · Vietnam Investment Review

Compeq holds capex above NT$20bn for three years, funding Thailand high-end capacity

Compeq plans to sustain capital expenditure above NT$20 billion (about $612.7 million) for three consecutive years from 2026 to 2028, funding process upgrades and high-end capacity expansion at its Taiwan, mainland China and Thailand plants.

📍 Thailand · Status: multi-year capex programme

Thailand's share of high-end PCB output rises, giving regional EMS buyers a domestic alternative for advanced boards.

Nov 2026 · BigGo Finance

Inari Amertron adds Penang RF factory space, lifting floor area 70%

Inari Amertron is acquiring leasehold industrial land and a factory building in the Bayan Lepas Free Trade Zone for RM22.8 million, lifting its Penang RF manufacturing gross floor area from 283,000 sq ft to 500,000 sq ft.

📍 Penang, Malaysia · Status: acquisition for capacity flexibility

Adds RF front-end capacity in Malaysia at a time of rising demand; gives buyers a second regional RF source alongside China and the Philippines.

India electrical appliances QCO 2026 tightens BIS certification gate

India's Safety of Household, Commercial and Similar Electrical Appliances (Quality Control) Order, 2026 makes BIS certification a precondition for market access — no certification, no India market entry for covered appliances.

BREAKINGWistron

Wistron injects additional $24.5m into Vietnam server operations

Wistron has injected a further $24.5 million into its Vietnam subsidiary, adding to roughly $300 million invested in the country since 2019, with the funds directed at expanding server production capacity.

The move follows board approvals for up to $59.05 million in additional Vietnam subsidiary investment and sits alongside $146 million allocated to equipment at its Westport, Texas plant — a two-track US-Vietnam capacity build-out.

Sourcing Implication

Vietnam's server ODM capacity is scaling fast; procurement teams should qualify Vietnamese server assembly as a China-plus-one node for rack-scale orders.

03 Nov 2026 · InvestVietnam · source
Luxshare

Luxshare-ICT adds 9m-unit VR line in Bac Ninh, lifting total capacity to 311m

Luxshare-ICT is expanding production scale in Vietnam with a new VR goggle line rated at 9 million units annually, part of a plan to raise total annual capacity across its Bac Ninh factories from 257 million to 311 million items.

The expansion carries total investment capital of $520 million and requires recruiting 11,220 additional workers to reach the new production volume.

Strategic Read

Vietnam is consolidating as the primary non-China base for VR/AR assembly; component suppliers should plan local inventory buffers accordingly.

Nov 2026 · The Investor · source
Foxconn

Foxconn targets November 2026 full-scale output on new Bac Ninh lines

Foxconn planned to install new production lines at its Bac Ninh factory between May and August 2026, run trial operations in September and begin full-scale production in November 2026, with humanoid robot production among the planned activities.

The company has committed around $4 billion in Vietnam since its first investment in 2007 and employs roughly 130,000 workers there, and has separately announced an additional $58.32 million investment in Fushan Technology (Vietnam).

Market Signal

Foxconn's Vietnam footprint is moving up the value curve from assembly toward automation and robotics — a signal for component and tooling suppliers to follow.

Nov 2026 · VOV · source

India

Manufacturing · PLI & Policy · Supply Chain · OEM/EMS
BREAKINGLG

LG Electronics India discloses phased Sri City investment in 5 Nov filing

LG Electronics India said in a 5 November 2026 filing that investments at its Sri City plant will be made in phases based on operational scale, equipment and technology needs, and did not disclose a rupee investment amount in the filing.

The 247-acre Sri City facility — LG's third India plant after Greater Noida and Pune — is a roughly ₹5,000 crore ($600 million) project targeting production start by December 2026 for air conditioners, compressors, refrigerators and washing machines, with around 1,495 direct jobs.

Strategic Read

Phased capex language signals LG is keeping Sri City flexible on product mix; component suppliers should expect staged qualification rather than a single ramp.

05 Nov 2026 · Multibagg · source
BREAKINGvivo / Dixon

India approves vivo–Dixon smartphone manufacturing joint venture

The Indian government approved the joint venture between Dixon Technologies India and vivo Mobile India under Press Note 3 of 2020, clearing the 51:49, Dixon-controlled structure for smartphone manufacturing.

The JV is expected to add 20–22 million units of annual capacity at full run rate and generate around ₹30,000 crore in revenue, with production starting in the December quarter of FY27 and backward integration into camera and display modules.

Sourcing Implication

A cleared vivo–Dixon JV gives buyers a compliant, India-domiciled smartphone manufacturing route with localised module supply over time.

04 Nov 2026 · Developing Telecoms · source
Dixon

Dixon sets 60m-unit FY28 and 70m-unit FY29 mobile volume targets

Dixon Technologies executives outlined multi-year mobile volume expansion targets of 60 million units by FY28 and 70 million units by FY29, supported by its joint ventures and growing component manufacturing capabilities.

Capacity is expected to rise from the current 70 million units to 180–190 million units over the next few years, with the company also targeting servers and data-centre segments.

Market Signal

Dixon's volume roadmap implies a multi-year pull for locally sourced components — a planning cue for Indian enclosure, display and camera module suppliers.

Nov 2026 · Sahi · source

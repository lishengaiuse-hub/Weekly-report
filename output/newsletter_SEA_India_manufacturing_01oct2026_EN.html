🇻🇳 Vietnam — Decree 371/2026/ND-CP issued 25 September 2026

Vietnam issued Decree No. 371/2026/ND-CP on 25 September 2026, detailing the recruitment and management of Vietnamese workers working for foreign organisations and individuals in Vietnam. Status: Decree issued 25 September 2026
Compliance alert: Foreign-invested manufacturers hiring Vietnamese workers must align recruitment and management processes with the new decree.
Source: LuatVietnam

🇮🇩 Indonesia — TKDN weighting reform in force since 11 December 2025

Indonesia's revamped TKDN regulation shifted the local-content calculation from a cost-production model to a weighting system. Companies producing goods in their own Indonesian factories and employing Indonesian citizens for at least half their workforce receive an automatic TKDN of 25 percent. Research and development activities are recognised as an additional contribution of up to 20 percent TKDN.

Status: Effective 11 December 2025
Compliance alert: For 4G/5G smartphones, TKDN certification remains a market-access gate — budget 2–5 months for audit and verification.
Source: Mori Hamada

🇲🇾 Malaysia — New Incentive Framework operational from 1 March 2026

Malaysia's New Incentive Framework (NIF) shifts to an outcome-based, performance-driven model aligned with national development priorities and global tax reforms. Manufacturing sector applications are open, with mechanisms including Special Tax Relief and Investment Tax Allowance at varying rates and durations based on company commitments and location. The framework is funded through an RM1 billion strategic investment fund.

Status: Manufacturing applications open since 1 March 2026
Source: Rödl & Partner

🇮🇳 India — ECMS outlay raised to ₹40,000 crore in Union Budget 2026–27

The Electronics Components Manufacturing Scheme received an enhanced outlay of ₹40,000 crore in the Union Budget 2026–27. The scheme covers multi-layer PCBs, camera modules, display drivers, power semiconductors, passive components and precision mechanical parts, supporting eligible manufacturers through turnover-linked, capex-linked and hybrid incentives at 5–10 percent on qualifying production for 5–8 years.

Status: Enhanced outlay in force for FY2026–27
Source: Press Information Bureau

🇹🇭 Thailand — BOI incentives for electronics and EEC projects

Thailand's Board of Investment offers qualifying EEC projects up to 100 percent foreign ownership, corporate income tax exemptions of up to 15 years, import duty exemptions, matching grants and a reduced personal income tax rate of 17 percent. The BOI also offers technology upgrade promotion for existing promoted manufacturers investing in AI systems, providing an additional 3 years of CIT exemption. Applications for smart electrical appliances and smart electronics incentives must be submitted by the last working day of 2026.

Status: Applications open, deadline last working day of 2026
Source: Thailand BOI

CSA has also concluded its public consultation on cybersecurity licensing framework updates, with key changes including mandatory certification requirements, extended five-year licence validity and streamlined notification processes, expected to come into force by end-2027.

Policy

Indonesia's TKDN weighting reform grants automatic 25% local content to in-country producers

Indonesia's revamped TKDN regulation shifted the local-content calculation from a cost-production model to a weighting system. Companies producing goods in their own Indonesian factories and employing Indonesian citizens for at least half their workforce receive an automatic TKDN of 25 percent. Research and development activities are recognised as an additional contribution of up to 20 percent TKDN.

The reform aims to simplify the TKDN calculation methodology, streamline the certification process and promote investment in innovation that fulfils local content requirements through technology development. For 4G/5G smartphones, TKDN certification remains the primary market-access gate, with data preparation taking 1–3 months and audit plus verification a further 1–2 months.

Compliance Alert

Budget 2–5 months and IDR 5–15 million in government fees per product for TKDN certification — the R&D credit is now worth structuring into local entity design.

Date: Effective 11 December 2025 · Mori Hamada

India's ECMS outlay raised to ₹40,000 crore covering PCBs, camera modules and passives

The Electronics Components Manufacturing Scheme received an enhanced outlay of ₹40,000 crore in the Union Budget 2026–27, reflecting a strong policy commitment to expanding domestic production capacity. The scheme covers multi-layer PCBs, camera modules, display drivers, power semiconductors, passive components and precision mechanical parts.

ECMS supports eligible electronics component manufacturers through turnover-linked, capex-linked and hybrid incentives at 5–10 percent on qualifying production for 5–8 years, with higher incentives for capital-intensive, high-difficulty components. For Target Segment D, eligible supply-chain ecosystem and capital equipment projects may qualify for capex-linked support.

Compliance Alert

ECMS is the higher-value opportunity versus assembly PLI — lower competition, longer incentive windows and direct alignment with India's component indigenisation goal.

India AC and appliance prices rise 5–8% from 1 October on component costs

ACs, TVs, washing machines and other appliances became costlier from 1 October, with prices expected to rise 5–8 percent ahead of the festive season. This is the industry's third price hike in 2026, driven by higher component costs. Leading brands including Haier, Blue Star, Daikin, LG and Voltas are affected.

The price action comes as India's air conditioner market share leaders Voltas, Daikin and Blue Star face margin pressure, with multiple AC-focused stocks making fresh yearly lows on cautious sector sentiment.

Market Signal

Three component-driven price hikes in one year signals structural input cost inflation in Indian appliance manufacturing — factor into 2027 landed-cost models.

Blue Star targets 1.8 million AC units and 14.3% market share by FY26

Blue Star Ltd plans to expand AC production to 1.8 million units, aiming for a 14.3 percent market share by FY26. The company's current production capacity is around 1.4 million room ACs. The expansion is gradual and involves advanced automation technologies and Internet of Things integration.

Managing Director B Thiagarajan said India is set to add approximately 450 million middle-class consumers by 2030, with the room AC market at an inflection point. Positive trends in the housing sector, renewable energy adoption and rural economy growth are expected to shape the industry's future.

Strategic Read

Indian room AC capacity is expanding ahead of demand — compressor, motor and heat exchanger component suppliers should align capacity with the 1.8 million unit target.

Embraco compressors to be manufactured in India at 6 million units annual capacity

Nidec Global Appliance will manufacture Embraco compressors for residential and commercial refrigeration in India, alongside electronic components, at a new factory with an annual production capacity of 6 million units. The facility is set to begin operations in the second half of 2026.

Alberto Casnati, president of Nidec Global Appliance, said the expansion underscores the company's commitment to meeting growing demand for refrigeration solutions in India and strengthens its presence in the region.

Sourcing Implication

India-origin compressor capacity at 6 million units gives appliance makers a domestic alternative to imported compressors for residential and commercial refrigeration.

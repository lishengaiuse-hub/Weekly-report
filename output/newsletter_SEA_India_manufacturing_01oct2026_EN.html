Core Component Supply Chain Update

PCB · Display · Camera · Battery · Passive · Cables · LED · Compressors/Motors · Touch/Glass

Battery Cells — BYD Vietnam & Sunwoda Thailand

BYD has broken ground on a $130 million battery plant in Vietnam targeting 3 GWh annual capacity in the first phase. Separately, Thailand's Commission on the National Competitiveness Enhancement for Targeted Industries approved a Sunwoda Electronic unit investment of more than $1 billion in EV and energy-storage battery cell production, with the first factory in Chonburi province.

📍 Vietnam & Chonburi, Thailand · status: groundbreaking / approved

Sourcing implication: Regional cell capacity reduces dependence on China-origin cells for ASEAN-assembled consumer and mobility products.

Connectors & Cables — Molex Hung Yen

Molex opened the initial building phase of its expanded 250,000-square-metre manufacturing plant in Thang Long Industrial Park II, Hung Yen, Vietnam, on 17 September 2026, supporting high-speed data center customers. The site is expected to grow to 8,000 employees by end-2026; Molex has operated in Vietnam for 15 years.

📍 Hung Yen, Vietnam · status: phase one opened

Sourcing implication: Expanded Vietnamese connector capacity supports data-center and consumer interconnect demand without China concentration.

Compressors & Motors — Nidec Global Appliance

Nidec Global Appliance is building a new manufacturing hub for Embraco compressors with production expected to begin in Q4 2026. Nidec's Embraco acquisition includes plants in Brazil, Uruguay, Slovakia and Luxembourg, with additional China entities to be acquired through a newly created holding company.

📍 Global network · status: production start Q4 2026

Sourcing implication: New Embraco compressor capacity affects refrigeration appliance BOM sourcing timelines into 2027.

Touch / Cover Glass — Biel Crystal & Corning

Biel Crystal operates production facilities in Vietnam including a Hai Duong Province base that commenced operations in Q3 2024 and a Bac Ninh site, manufacturing smart device glass, camera optical modules and automotive cover glass. Corning plans to begin Gorilla cover-glass production in India in the first half of 2026 in partnership with Optiemus, targeting exports.

📍 Hai Duong & Bac Ninh, Vietnam; India · status: operating / ramping

Sourcing implication: Cover-glass finishing capacity in Vietnam and India reduces single-country exposure for device front-glass supply.

Thailand secures over $30.5B in electronics investment since 2023

Thailand has attracted over $30.5 billion in electronics investment since 2023, with more than one-third concentrated in printed circuit boards and electronic components. The investment momentum is being showcased at the Thailand Electronics Circuit Asia (THECA 2026) summit at Bangkok's BITEC exhibition center, convening more than 300 global tech enterprises and 7,000 international delegates across 40 nations.

China accounts for 44% of Thailand's total manufacturing FDI, with Chinese EV makers using Thailand as an export base. The BOI has highlighted AI-linked investment in optical transceivers, PCBs, high-capacity hard disk drives, AI servers and smart electronics.

Sourcing Implication

BYD Electronics breaks ground on $183.7M northern Vietnam plant

BYD Electronics began construction on its $183.7 million plant in Phu Tho province, northern Vietnam, on 28 September 2026. The expansion adds new product lines including USB chargers, in-car fast wireless chargers, robotic pool cleaners, lithium batteries, 4G/5G chips, GPS modules for smartphones and tablets, gimbal stabilizers, ultra-light consumer drones and graphic processing boards.

BYD has also requested approval to manufacture 18.72 million metal casings for Apple tablets annually and to repair up to 600,000 Apple iPads per year, including devices produced in Vietnam and at BYD's global facilities. The first phase covers over 473,000 square feet targeting 3 GWh annual capacity, with a second phase expanding to 1 million square feet and 6 GWh.

Sourcing Implication

BYD's Phu Tho build-out adds Vietnamese capacity for Apple tablet casings and accessory electronics, deepening non-China supply options.

Vietnam issues Decree 371/2026/ND-CP on foreign-organisation worker recruitment

Decree No. 371/2026/ND-CP was issued on25 September 2026, detailing the recruitment and management of Vietnamese workers working for foreign organisations and individuals in Vietnam. The decree forms part of Vietnam's broader shift from volume-based to value-based FDI attraction.

Vietnam's electronics development strategy to 2030 targets establishing the country as a global hub for chip design, manufacturing, packaging and testing, with Decree No. 182/2024/NĐ-CP supporting high-tech projects through cost-based support.

India appliance prices rise 5–8% from October 1 as input costs climb

Prices of air conditioners, LED televisions, washing machines and other home appliances in India increased by 5% to 8% from 1 October 2026, as manufacturers passed on rising input costs to consumers ahead of the festive season. The increase marks the industry's third round of price hikes in 2026.

Manufacturers cited higher prices for copper, aluminium, steel and crude oil derivatives, along with increased freight costs and currency volatility. Leading brands including Haier, Blue Star, Daikin, LG and Voltas are affected.

Market Signal

Third 2026 price hike signals sustained input-cost pressure across India's appliance BOM, with festive-season demand absorbing the pass-through.

Tata Electronics weighs ₹2,000 crore components investment under incentive scheme

Tata Electronics is likely to invest ₹2,000 crore for electronics components manufacturing under the ₹23,000 crore incentive scheme launched by the Indian government, according to a 25 September 2026 report. The move would extend Tata's manufacturing footprint beyond assembly into higher-value component production.

b-policy

India ECMS outlay enhanced to ₹40,000 crore in Union Budget 2026–27

The Electronics Components Manufacturing Scheme outlay was enhanced to ₹40,000 crore in the Union Budget 2026–27, reflecting a strong policy commitment to expanding domestic production capacity. The scheme covers multi-layer PCBs, camera modules, display drivers, power semiconductors, passive components and precision mechanical parts.

Eligible electronics component manufacturers are supported through turnover-linked, capex-linked and hybrid incentives, with 5–10% on qualifying production over 5–8 years and higher incentives for capital-intensive, high-difficulty components.

Compliance Alert

Component makers should assess ECMS eligibility now, given longer incentive windows and lower competition versus assembly PLI.

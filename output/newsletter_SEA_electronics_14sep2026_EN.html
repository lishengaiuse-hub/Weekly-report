Policy Focus

Regulation, certification and tariff changes affecting SEA market access

🇮🇩 Indonesia — Minister of Trade Regulation No. 19 of 2026 on e-commerce

On 8 June 2026, Indonesia's Minister of Trade enacted Regulation No. 19 of 2026 on Electronic Commerce, replacing Minister of Trade Regulation No. 31 of 2023. The new regulation expands the categories of businesses subject to PMSE rules, adds mandatory protections for sellers, introduces algorithmic obligations for marketplace platforms, tightens conditions for cross-border sellers, and establishes Indonesia's first trade-level framework for artificial intelligence in commerce. Platforms now carry direct enforcement responsibilities.

Status: In force since 8 June 2026 · Applies to domestic and foreign platforms
Compliance alert: Foreign platforms, sellers and digital commerce participants must reassess PMSE operations against seller licensing, cross-border trade, fee transparency, complaint handling, domestic product prioritisation and AI governance requirements.

🇮🇩 Indonesia — TKDN local content requirements for electronics

Indonesia's TKDN local content requirement mandates minimum local components for electronics sold in Indonesia. Operators (Telkomsel, Indosat, XL, Tri) block IMEIs of devices without complete certification, meaning customers cannot use Indonesian SIM cards. E-commerce platforms including Tokopedia, Shopee, Lazada and Blibli increasingly require proof of TKDN before listing.

Status: Enforced · Non-compliance risks device deactivation and legal penalties
Compliance alert: Devices found sold without proper TKDN face customs and tax clawback risk on previously claimed preferences.

🇲🇾 Malaysia — MCMC new communication equipment standards

Malaysia's MCMC has set implementation dates for two communication equipment standards. All subsequent related equipment submitted for certification must comply with the two new standards. MCMC offers a six-month grace period, with the new regulations fully enforced starting 5 November 2026.

Status: Grace period active · Full enforcement 5 November 2026
Compliance alert: Online shopping platforms should update internal listing policies to require proof of SIRIM certification from local sellers; consumers can verify via the CYL app or SIRIM eComM.

🇸🇬 Singapore — IMDA moves residential gateways to RG-SEC Issue 2

Singapore's IMDA is transitioning residential gateways to RG-SEC Issue 2 and CLS Level 2 cybersecurity requirements, with new-model registration changing 8 December 2026 and full local-market compliance required by 31 December 2027. Under CLS Level 2, manufacturers must ensure residential routers incorporate stronger security measures such as secure communications, secure storage of sensitive data and robust authentication mechanisms.

Status: New-model registration change 8 Dec 2026 · Full compliance by end-2027
Compliance alert: CSA has concluded its public consultation on cybersecurity licensing framework updates; key changes include mandatory certification requirements, extended five-year licence validity and streamlined notification processes, expected in force by end-2027.

🌏 Tariff environment — Vietnam, Malaysia, Thailand, Philippines

Vietnam's electronics exports to the US carry a 0% MFN base duty under ITA or preferential rates, with an effective rate of roughly 0.5% after merchandise processing and harbour maintenance fees on a reference $50,000 CIF shipment. Section 232 tariffs (25% steel / 10% aluminium content) may apply to Vietnam electronics with steel or aluminium content. The Administration signed a framework keeping Vietnam's reciprocal rate at 20% while potentially placing 0% duty on some Annex III articles; Malaysia's framework maintains a 19% reciprocal rate with 0% extended to many Annex III articles.

Status: Frameworks signed · Section 301 excess capacity investigations threatened across ASEAN
Compliance alert: Section 301 excess capacity investigations cover Bangladesh, Cambodia, China, the EU, India, Indonesia, Japan, Malaysia, Mexico, Norway, Singapore, South Korea, Switzerland, Taiwan, Thailand and Vietnam.

Policy

MCMC sets November 2026 enforcement for two communication equipment standards

Malaysia's MCMC has set implementation dates for two communication equipment standards, with all subsequently submitted related equipment required to comply. A six-month grace period applies, with full enforcement beginning 5 November 2026. The government encourages applicants to achieve compliance during the grace period.

SIRIM certification remains mandatory for consumer electronics in Malaysia. The certificate holder must be a local business entity registered with the Companies Commission of Malaysia (SSM) and must possess suitable premises, such as a registered office or warehouse, to store and distribute certified goods.

Compliance Alert

Importers without a locally registered SSM entity and qualifying premises cannot hold SIRIM certification — a structural barrier for cross-border sellers ahead of the November deadline.

Market Data

SEA smartphone market falls 23% in 2Q26 to lowest level since 2014

Southeast Asia's smartphone market saw a sharp downturn in 2Q26, with shipments falling 23% to their lowest level since 2014 as entry-level supply declined and average selling prices increased. Samsung gained market share despite raising prices, with the Galaxy A07 and A17 repriced upward after launch, reversing the typical post-launch price decline pattern. This lifted Samsung's share of the overall $200–299 segment from 18% in 2Q25 to 32% in 2Q26.

Xiaomi posted the second-largest ASP increase among the top five vendors at 43.5%, while shipments declined. Globally, Xiaomi recorded the steepest decline among the top five brands with shipments falling 26% year-on-year in Q2 2026, and vivo shipments declined 21% year-on-year as pressure on lower price segments outweighed resilience in its mid-to-premium portfolio.

Market Signal

The overstocking and subsidy-driven volume strategies that defined SEA's smartphone market in past years have reversed — understocked channels now let vendors enforce pricing discipline.

2026 · Omdia / Counterpoint Research · The Fast Mode

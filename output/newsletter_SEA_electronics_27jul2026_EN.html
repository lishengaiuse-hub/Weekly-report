🌍 Global Industry Shifts — SEA Impact

Chinese appliance brands gain ground in Southeast Asia

Chinese brands (Midea, Haier, TCL, Hisense) are gaining share in TVs and air conditioners across SEA, driven by pricing and features appealing to younger buyers. In Thailand, Chinese share of home laundry appliances rose to 23.2% from 15.3%.

→ Downstream SEA impact: Intensifying price competition for Japanese and Korean brands; increased production commitments in Thailand and Vietnam.
Source: Nikkei Asia, 22 Jun 2026

Global smartphone market down 4% in Q2 2026; Apple and Samsung buck trend

Omdia reports global smartphone shipments fell 4% YoY in Q2 2026 due to memory crisis and rising component costs. Samsung and Apple grew share, while Xiaomi, OPPO, and vivo declined.

→ Downstream SEA impact: SEA ASP hit record $349 in Q1 2026 (+19% YoY); volume down 9% but value up 8%.
Source: Omdia, 13 Jul 2026

China+1 strategy accelerates; Vietnam, India, Mexico lead alternatives

Manufacturers are diversifying from China with parallel footprints in Vietnam, India, and Mexico. Vietnam benefits from 10% Section 122 tariff vs. China's combined ~35% rate.

→ Downstream SEA impact: Vietnam and Malaysia see increased FDI in electronics assembly and component manufacturing.
Source: DocShipper, Jul 2026

MLCC prices rise 15-35% as Murata, Samsung Electro-Mechanics, Yageo hike quotes

High-capacitance MLCCs for AI servers and automotive see strongest increases. Yageo began broadest capacitor hike in years on 1 July 2026.

→ Downstream SEA impact: Higher BOM costs for SEA-based smartphone and appliance assemblers; potential margin pressure.
Source: TrendForce, 1 Jul 2026

📜 Policy Focus

Indonesia: New e-commerce regulation Permendag 19/2026

Minister of Trade Regulation No. 19/2026 replaces Permendag 31/2023, imposing stricter compliance on platforms (PPMSE) and merchants, including seller licensing, cross-border trade rules, domestic product prioritization, and AI governance.

Effective: 8 June 2026
⚠️ Foreign platforms must reassess PMSE operations; TKDN certification increasingly required for electronics listings.

Malaysia: Integrated crackdown on non-compliant electrical products

Government to launch Special Compliance Operation targeting import and sale of electrical products failing safety standards, including those sold via e-commerce.

Announced: 23 July 2026
⚠️ Importers and distributors must ensure SIRIM certification; enforcement expected to intensify.

Singapore: Cybersecurity labelling for routers raised to CLS Level 2 by 2027

CSA and IMDA will raise mandatory cybersecurity requirements for residential routers from CLS Level 1 to Level 2 by end 2027.

Announced: Jul 2026
⚠️ Router manufacturers and importers must prepare for stricter cybersecurity certification.

Vietnam: US tariff at 10% Section 122; Section 301 replacement in motion

Vietnamese goods enter US at 10% baseline plus normal product duty. Section 122 authority expires ~24 July 2026; Section 301 replacement being prepared.

Current: 10% · Under review
⚠️ SEA electronics exporters should monitor tariff developments closely.

Policy

Indonesia issues new e-commerce regulation Permendag 19/2026

Minister of Trade Regulation No. 19/2026 replaces Permendag 31/2023, imposing stricter compliance on e-commerce platforms and merchants. Key changes include seller licensing, cross-border trade rules, domestic product prioritization, AI governance, and fee transparency.

E-commerce platforms (Tokopedia, Shopee, Lazada, Blibli) increasingly require TKDN proof for electronics listings.

Compliance Alert

Foreign platforms must reassess operations; TKDN certification critical for smartphone and electronics sales.

17 Jul 2026 · Walalangi & Partners · W&P Newsletter
Policy

Indonesia TKDN policy reform balances domestic industry and global competitiveness

Indonesia's TKDN reform introduces flexibility without compromising national interests. Devices without complete certification cannot be activated on Indonesian networks (IMEI blocking) or sold legally on e-commerce platforms.

Tax/duty clawback risk exists for non-compliant devices.

Compliance Alert

Ensure TKDN certification before shipping to Indonesia; IMEI registration mandatory.

Core Component Supply Chain Update

Plants and facilities located in SEA / India

Foxconn Bac Ninh humanoid robot lines move to full-scale production

Foxconn plans to install new humanoid robot production lines at its Bac Ninh factory between May and August 2026, run trial operations in September, and begin full-scale production in November 2026. Bac Ninh is Foxconn's most important manufacturing base in Vietnam, with cumulative investment above $4 billion since 2007 and a workforce exceeding 100,000.

📍 Bac Ninh, Vietnam · Status: full-scale production from November 2026

Robotics assembly inside an existing electronics cluster signals a shift up the value chain for northern Vietnam — watch for local sourcing of actuators, harnesses and precision parts.

Nov 2026 · VOV English

Luxshare-ICT to add 9 million VR units of capacity in Bac Ninh

Luxshare-ICT plans to lift annual capacity across its Bac Ninh factories from 257 million to 311 million items, adding 9 million virtual reality products. Total investment capital is $520 million, with recruitment of 11,220 additional workers taking the site headcount to roughly 31,440.

📍 Bac Ninh, Vietnam · Status: capacity expansion

VR/AR volume concentration in northern Vietnam deepens the local optics, acoustic and structural-parts supplier base — a sourcing alternative to China for wearable assemblies.

Nov 2026 · The Investor

Goertek seeks approval for $570 million Bac Ninh expansion

Goertek is set to invest $570 million into expanding its operations in Bac Ninh, pending approvals from local authorities. The Vietnamese subsidiary manufactures consumer electronics including AirPods, smartwatches and VR/AR devices.

📍 Bac Ninh, Vietnam · Status: pending local approval

Acoustic and wearable assembly capacity continues to migrate north — component makers should align logistics with the Bac Ninh–Bac Giang corridor.

India clears Dixon–Vivo joint venture under Press Note 3

The Indian government has approved the joint venture between Dixon Technologies India and vivo Mobile India, cleared under Press Note 3 of 2020. Dixon holds 51% and vivo 49%; MeitY approved vivo's investment in the new subsidiary.

Status: Approved — JV operationalisation in progress

Indonesia

Manufacturing · OEM/EMS
Pegatron

Pegatron inaugurates AI- and 5G-powered smart factory in Batam

Pegatron has officially inaugurated its latest smart manufacturing facility in Batam, Indonesia, adding 5G-enabled, AI-driven production capacity to the Batam industrial cluster. The site sits alongside existing EMS operations in the island's free-trade zone.

Batam continues to attract contract manufacturers seeking tariff-resilient, Singapore-adjacent capacity for consumer electronics assembly and export.

Sourcing Implication

Buyers diversifying out of China should evaluate Batam for box-build and system assembly, with Singapore serving as the regional logistics and engineering hub.

Thailand

Manufacturing · Supply Chain
HaierBreaking

Chinese appliance majors consolidate Thailand "iron triangle" in Chonburi

Haier's Chonburi air-conditioner base carries a planned annual capacity of 6 million units, the largest AC manufacturing base by a Chinese brand in Southeast Asia. Midea's Chonburi smart factory is expected to reach 6 million units annually next year, while Hisense has invested THB 4.7 billion in a Chonburi smart manufacturing park targeting refrigerators and washing machines with capacity above 2.6 million units.

Haier is also expanding factories in Chonburi and Prachin Buri, and Midea has invested over THB 2.2 billion in a new air-conditioner plant. The clustering is reshaping Thailand's white-goods supply base.

Strategic Read

Thailand is becoming the ASEAN white-goods export platform — component suppliers for compressors, motors, heat exchangers and controls should localise to serve all four majors.

Vietnam

Manufacturing & Supply Chain · OEM/EMS
Foxconn

Foxconn commits $383 million to Bac Ninh factory

Foxconn is pouring $383 million into its Bac Ninh factory, reinforcing the province as its most important manufacturing base in Vietnam. The group has invested over $4 billion in the country since 2007 and employs more than 100,000 workers.

Bac Ninh hosts Foxconn's core electronics assembly and component operations, with additional capacity in Bac Giang, Hanoi, Quang Ninh and Nghe An.

Market Signal

Continued capital deployment in the north confirms Vietnam as the primary China+1 destination for consumer electronics assembly through the decade.

Nov 2026 · Vietnam Investment Review
Foxconn

Foxconn adds $287 million to Bac Ninh PCBA and communications capacity

The scheme covers about 49.6 hectares in Quang Chau Industrial Park and focuses on producing electronic components, PCBA circuit boards, computers and communications equipment. The additional $287 million builds on Foxconn's existing Bac Ninh footprint.

PCBA and communications equipment capacity in the north supports both Apple-linked and networking customers.

Sourcing Implication

PCBA capacity in Vietnam reduces the need to ship bare boards from China for final assembly — evaluate local board houses for volume programs.

India

Manufacturing · PLI & Policy · Supply Chain · OEM/EMS
Haier

Haier India plans ₹3,500 crore third manufacturing facility

Haier Appliances India plans to invest ₹3,500 crore over three to four years to establish its third manufacturing facility in western or southern India, with the location yet to be finalised. The investment is expected to generate around 12,000 direct and indirect jobs.

Haier currently operates plants in Pune and Greater Noida and targets ₹14,500 crore in revenue by 2026. The new facility will primarily serve the domestic market while catering to export opportunities.

Brand Watch

Haier's localisation deepens India white-goods competition — suppliers should position for the new site's component sourcing once the state is confirmed.

Nov 2026 · Projects Today
Foxconn

Foxconn lines up $1 billion smartphone display module plant near Chennai

Foxconn plans to invest $1 billion to establish a smartphone display module plant in Tamil Nadu near Chennai, acquiring about 500,000 square feet at the ESR Oragadam Industrial & Logistics Park. The facility will be Foxconn's first display module plant in India, primarily supplying Apple's iPhone, with Pegatron and Tata Electronics also able to access output.

The project is expected to create around 14,000 jobs once operational and marks India's move from assembly into deeper localisation of high-value electronics manufacturing.

Strategic Read

Display module localisation closes a key import gap for Indian handset assembly — panel and backlight suppliers should plan India-side capacity.

Nov 2026 · Global SMT
Apple

Apple ecosystem in India scales toward 300,000 workers by 2028

Apple's India manufacturing ecosystem employs about 150,000 workers and is expected to expand to over 300,000 by 2028. The company's operational footprint includes five major assembly facilities, three operated by Tata Group, with Foxconn leading total production and Tata Electronics overtaking Foxconn in iPhone exports.

Apple aims to shift the majority of iPhones sold in the US to Indian production lines by end-2026, supported by the production-linked incentive scheme.

Market Signal

India's Apple value chain is now large enough to anchor tier-2 supplier investment — enclosures, connectors and acoustics are the next localisation wave.

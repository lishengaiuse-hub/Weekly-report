August 2026 Global Electronics Sentiment Survey: Supply Chain Tightening

Policy Focus

Indonesia: Permendag 19/2026 Overhauls E-Commerce Regulation

Minister of Trade Regulation No. 19 of 2026 replaces Permendag 31/2023, expanding regulatory coverage, strengthening consumer protection, and introducing Indonesia's first AI governance rules in a commercial context. Platforms become directly responsible for seller compliance. Five-tier sanctions framework backs enforcement.

Status: Enacted 8 June 2026 · Compliance mandatory
⚠️ Foreign platforms and sellers must reassess PMSE operations: seller licensing, cross-border trade, fee transparency, domestic product prioritization, AI governance.

Malaysia: SIRIM Certification Enforcement from November 2026

MCMC offers six-month grace period; new regulations fully enforced starting November 5, 2026. Regulations took effect June 1, 2026. Online platforms must require proof of SIRIM certification from local sellers.

Status: Full enforcement 5 Nov 2026
⚠️ Importers and online sellers must secure SIRIM certification before November deadline.

Singapore: CSA Raises Cybersecurity Labelling for Residential Routers

CSA will raise mandatory cybersecurity requirements for routers to CLS Level 2. Manufacturers must ensure secure communications, secure storage of sensitive data, and robust authentication. Certification mandatory for Wi-Fi routers (Level 1), voluntary for most consumer IoT.

Status: Level 2 requirement announced; implementation timeline pending

Philippines: Electronics Tariff Structure 2026

Philippines maintains 0% import duty on ICs (HS 8542) and laptops (HS 8471) under ITA. TVs at 10% MFN / 0% ATIGA. VAT at 12%. Both Philippines and Vietnam face identical US tariff structures: 0% MFN + 10% Section 122 = 10% total.

Policy

Singapore CSA to Raise Cybersecurity Labelling Requirements for Residential Routers

CSA will raise mandatory cybersecurity requirements for routers to CLS Level 2. Under Level 2, manufacturers must ensure residential routers incorporate secure communications, secure storage of sensitive data, and robust authentication mechanisms.

Certification is currently mandatory for Wi-Fi routers (Level 1), voluntary for most consumer IoT devices. Mutual recognition agreements exist with Finland, Germany, UK, and South Korea.

Permendag 19/2026: Indonesia's E-Commerce Overhaul with AI Governance

Minister of Trade Regulation No. 19 of 2026 replaces Permendag 31/2023, significantly expanding what e-commerce platforms (domestic and foreign) must do. Platforms must actively oversee merchant activities, pricing practices, consumer-facing information, and product traceability.

The regulation widens categories of businesses subject to PMSE rules, tightens conditions for cross-border sellers, mandates algorithmic changes for domestic product promotion, and introduces Indonesia's first AI governance rules in a commercial context. A five-tier sanctions framework backs enforcement.

Policy

TKDN Enforcement: IMEI Blocking and Platform Listing Requirements

Operators (Telkomsel, Indosat, XL, Tri) block IMEIs of devices without complete certification. Customers cannot use Indonesian SIM cards on uncertified devices. E-commerce platforms (Tokopedia, Shopee, Lazada, Blibli) increasingly require proof of TKDN before listing.

Customs and tax authorities can demand reclamation of preferential duties if devices are found sold without proper TKDN certification.

Compliance Alert

Device importers must secure TKDN certification before distribution; IMEI blocking is actively enforced.

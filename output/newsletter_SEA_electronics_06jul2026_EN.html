📜 Policy Focus — Regulatory Changes Affecting CE

🇸🇬 Singapore — MELS Labeling Mandatory from 1 July 2026

Singapore's NEA enforces mandatory Minimum Energy Labelling Scheme (MELS) for ACs, fridges, dryers, TVs, and lighting. Importers must register with NEA and demonstrate MEPS compliance.

Effective: 1 July 2026 · Status: In force
Compliance Alert: Non-compliant appliances cannot be sold or imported; testing and registration lead times critical.

🇮🇩 Indonesia — TKDN Enforcement Tightens on E-Commerce

E-commerce platforms (Tokopedia, Shopee, Lazada, Blibli) increasingly require proof of TKDN before listing. Devices without complete certification face IMEI blocking by operators (Telkomsel, Indosat, XL, Tri). Customs can demand tax clawback for non-compliant devices.

Effective: Ongoing 2026 · Status: Increasingly enforced
Compliance Alert: Foreign brands must partner with local manufacturers or face legal and operational hurdles.

🇸🇬 Singapore — CSA Raises Router Cybersecurity to Level 2 by End-2027

CSA and IMDA will raise mandatory cybersecurity requirements for residential routers from CLS Level 1 to Level 2 by end-2027. Certifications valid for five years. Mutual recognition agreement with Japan for IoT cybersecurity signed March 2026.

Effective: End-2027 · Status: Consultation concluded; implementation pending
Compliance Alert: Manufacturers must incorporate secure communications, storage, and authentication for router designs.

🇻🇳 Vietnam — New Tech Lists Reshape Priority Sectors July 2026

Vietnam updates priority technology lists, affecting import tariffs and investment incentives for electronics manufacturing. Section 122 tariff on electronics from China expires July 24, 2026, making Vietnam a more attractive sourcing destination.

Effective: July 2026 · Status: Updated lists published
Strategic Read: Vietnam's 0% base tariff on electronics combined with expiring US Section 122 tariff enhances its export competitiveness.

🇲🇾 Malaysia — SIRIM, Service Tax, and Expat Policy Updates

Malaysia enforces RoHS 2.0 for E&E imports from March 2025. SIRIM mandates IPv6 reports for approvals from July 2025. Service tax and sales tax policy updates announced June 2026. MIDA revises expatriate employment categories with higher minimum salaries.

Effective: Various 2025-2026 · Status: In force
Compliance Alert: RoHS 2.0 compliance required for all E&E imports; expat hiring costs increase for manufacturing roles.

Policy

MELS Labeling Mandatory for Appliances from 1 July 2026

Singapore's NEA enforces mandatory Minimum Energy Labelling Scheme (MELS) for air conditioners, refrigerators, dryers, TVs, and lighting. Importers must register with NEA and demonstrate compliance with Minimum Energy Performance Standards (MEPS).

Energy Conservation Bill Amendment requires households and businesses to register imported regulated goods. Non-compliant appliances cannot be sold or imported.

Compliance Alert

All appliance importers must ensure NEA registration and MEPS compliance immediately; testing and certification lead times critical.

30 May 2026 · Sector Journal HQ · source
Policy

CSA Raises Router Cybersecurity to Level 2; Mutual Recognition with Japan

CSA and IMDA will raise mandatory cybersecurity requirements for residential routers from CLS Level 1 to Level 2 by end-2027. Requirements include secure communications, secure storage, and robust authentication. CSA signed MoC with Japan's METI for mutual recognition of IoT cybersecurity schemes on 18 March 2026.

Certifications valid for five years. Streamlined certification process for manufacturers serving both Singapore and Japan markets.

Compliance Alert

Router manufacturers must design for Level 2 security by end-2027; mutual recognition reduces duplicate testing for Japan-Singapore exports.

5 March 2026 · CSA Singapore · source

Policy

TKDN Enforcement Tightens: IMEI Blocking, E-Commerce Listing Restrictions

Indonesia enforces TKDN (local content) requirements for electronics. Operators Telkomsel, Indosat, XL, Tri block IMEIs of devices without complete certification. E-commerce platforms Tokopedia, Shopee, Lazada, Blibli increasingly require proof of TKDN before listing. Customs can demand tax clawback for non-compliant devices.

New regulation introduces filtering mechanism: only goods produced from industrial activities in Indonesia per KBLI classification can be certified. Imported raw materials excluded from TKDN calculation.

Compliance Alert

Foreign brands must partner with local manufacturers or face complete market access blockage; TKDN certification now prerequisite for e-commerce listing.

2026 · Kickrate · source

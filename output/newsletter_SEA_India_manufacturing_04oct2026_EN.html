Suntak Technology Breaks Ground on RMB 1.2 Billion Printed Circuit Board Base in Thailand

Suntak Technology initiated site construction on September 28, 2026, for its major printed circuit board production plant in Thailand. The development spans approximately 168 mu (112,000 square meters) and carries an initial capital expenditure of RMB 1.2 billion, forming the first phase of an overarching regional footprint strategy designed to scale up to RMB 10 billion across Southeast Asia.

Equipment installation and production commissioning are scheduled to conclude before the end of 2026. The industrial park will specialize in high-density interconnect (HDI) and high-layer rigid PCBs designed to serve high-mix electronics assemblies, telecom equipment, and server hardware, responding directly to hardware assembly lines shifting procurement orders away from mainland manufacturing hubs.

📍 Rayong / Chonburi Corridor, Thailand · Groundbreaking

Sourcing Implication: Hardware procurement teams managing HDI and multi-layer boards will secure localized bare-board sourcing within ASEAN by late 2026, lowering export tariff overheads and lead times for regional Tier-1 box-builders.

BYD Breaks Ground on $130 Million Dedicated Battery Manufacturing Facility in Vietnam

BYD officially broke ground on a $130 million energy storage and battery manufacturing plant in Vietnam on September 28, 2026. The new specialized complex will fabricate battery cells and packaged energy modules to support industrial energy systems, mobility hardware, and localized sub-assembly for consumer durables manufacturing chains operating within Southeast Asia.

The construction start represents an extension of BYD’s manufacturing diversification across northern Vietnam's electronics corridor. The investment adds critical domestic cell and power-pack capacity to a market previously dependent on imported modules from Chinese parent hubs, compressing customs transit windows and lowering balance-of-plant assembly costs.

📍 Northern Vietnam · Construction Underway

Strategic Read: Captive and merchant battery capacity within Vietnam reduces transpacific supply friction for regional electronics assemblers requiring high-capacity lithium-ion cells for smart systems and backup power units.

Meiko Electronics Breaks Ground on $500 Million Circuit Board Complex in Northern Vietnam

Japanese interconnect specialist Meiko Electronics broke ground on a $500 million electronic circuit facility in Vietnam on September 28, 2026. The large-scale manufacturing expansion reinforces Meiko’s production cluster in northern Vietnam, expanding output for advanced HDI circuit boards and surface-mount assemblies destined for global consumer device brands.

The capital injection expands high-spec electronic circuit manufacturing capacity to match rising local assembly demands from smartphone and consumer electronics plants. The plant incorporates dedicated cleanrooms and automated plating lines to deliver circuit components directly to neighboring Tier-1 final assembly operations.

📍 Northern Industrial Belt, Vietnam · Groundbreaking

Market Signal: Japanese precision component vendors are solidifying captive assembly clusters in northern Vietnam to match Samsung and Apple supply-chain consolidation demands without crossing borders.

Foxconn Commits $383 Million Expansion for Bac Ninh Circuit Board and Smart Component Facility

Hon Hai Precision Industry (Foxconn) confirmed on September 29, 2026, an additional investment of $383 million into its manufacturing base in Bac Ninh province, Vietnam. The new capital commitment focuses on expanding specialized production lines for high-density printed circuit boards, surface-mount sub-assemblies, and precision peripheral modules required across the contractor's consumer client accounts.

The capital addition builds on Foxconn's aggregate industrial footprint in northern Vietnam, where the company already operates facilities spanning Bac Ninh, Bac Giang, and Quang Ninh. The accelerated expenditure allows Foxconn to deepen upstream parts fabrication in-house, shifting Vietnam operations from pure final system assembly (FATP) toward high-margin circuit and board fabrication.

Strategic Read

Foxconn is steadily vertically integrating its Vietnamese base, diminishing component supply risks from mainland China while maintaining tight inventory turns for regional laptop and tablet assembly contracts.

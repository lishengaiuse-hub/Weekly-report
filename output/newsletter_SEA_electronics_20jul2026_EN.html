🌏 Global Industry Shifts — SEA Impact

Chinese brands dominate top-50 electronics list; Japanese incumbents pivot to data centres

China leads the top 50 global electronics and appliance brands in 2026 with 19 entries, followed by Japan (12) and the US (9). Nikkei reports that Panasonic, once dominant in home appliances, now turns to data-centre infrastructure. Samsung announced it would cease sales of all home appliance products in mainland China, reflecting continued contraction.

→ Downstream SEA impact: Chinese brands (Haier, Midea, Hisense, TCL) accelerate factory build-out in Thailand, Vietnam, Indonesia to serve regional and export markets.
Sources: Nikkei Asia (15 Jul 2026), Global Times (13 Mar 2026), LinkedIn/Bill Jia

China+1 strategy recalibrates SEA supply chains; Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia lead

Alibaba.com reports SEA consumer electronics exports surged 533% YoY, driven by smart home devices. The China+1 strategy is now a multi-hub production ecosystem. Indonesia is increasingly attractive for both manufacturing and domestic consumption.

→ Downstream SEA impact: EMS providers (Foxconn, Wistron, Pegatron) and component makers (PCB, display, battery) expand capacity across Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia.
Sources: Alibaba.com Seller Blog, YouTube/2026 Asia sourcing trends, DocShipper, IMARC Engineering

Global smartphone market down 4% in Q2 2026; SEA shipments decline 9% in Q1

Omdia reports global smartphone shipments fell 4% YoY in Q2 2026. In SEA, Q1 2026 shipments dropped 9% as vendors prioritised profitability over volume. Average selling prices hit a record high. Samsung was the only top-5 brand growing in SEA (+4% YoY).

→ Downstream SEA impact: Channel understocking enables price discipline; Xiaomi raised Redmi Note 15 4G price to RM799 in Malaysia.
Sources: Omdia (13 Jul 2026, 14 Jul 2026), Android Headlines (May 2026), IDC

🔧 Core Component Supply Chain Update

PCB/FPC — Zhen Ding trial production in Thailand; Meiko completes Vietnam plant

Zhen Ding Technology began trial production at its $2B Rayong plant in Thailand, adding 1.2M m² of any-layer HDI capacity for AI-server modules. Meiko Electronics completed its $200M multilayer-board factory in Vietnam, bringing 600,000 m² annual capacity online.

📍 Thailand, Vietnam · Status: Trial/Operational

Sourcing Implication

Additional HDI and multilayer PCB capacity in SEA eases supply constraints for consumer electronics and AI-server modules.

Display — Samsung Display invests $1.8B in new OLED module line in Vietnam

Samsung Display will build a new OLED assembly plant in Vietnam, processing ~10M 8.6th-generation OLED panels annually from 2026. The facility handles back-end assembly for laptops and tablets.

📍 Vietnam · Status: Announced, construction pending

Sourcing Implication

Strengthens Vietnam's position in IT OLED assembly; supports Apple MacBook transition to OLED.

Camera Modules — LG Innotek completes V3 factory in Vietnam; capacity to double

LG Innotek completed its new camera module factory (V3) in Hai Phong, Vietnam. The $1B investment will more than double production capacity. Vietnam is expected to account for 70-80% of LG Innotek's camera module output.

📍 Hai Phong, Vietnam · Status: Completed Jul 2026

Sourcing Implication

Major capacity addition for Apple iPhone camera module supply; price competition intensifies among module makers.

Battery — ATL (TDK) plans Malaysia cell factory; Malaysia starts domestic EV battery production

TDK announced its subsidiary ATL will open a battery cell factory in Malaysia to diversify production outside China. Separately, Malaysia began small-scale production of a domestically developed graphene-enhanced lithium-ion battery for EVs.

📍 Malaysia · Status: Planned / Initial production Jul 2026

Sourcing Implication

New consumer electronics battery cell capacity in Malaysia reduces reliance on China for smartphone and laptop batteries.

Passive Components — MLCC market enters K-shaped recovery; Murata raises prices 15-35%

Murata announced price increases of 15-35% effective April 2026 for AI-server, automotive, and RF MLCCs. Samsung Electro-Mechanics, Yageo, and Walsin followed. High-spec segments see strong demand; low-end remains weak.

📍 Global · Status: Price hike effective Apr 2026

Sourcing Implication

Procurement teams should secure allocation for high-capacitance MLCCs; expect longer lead times for automotive and AI-grade parts.

Compressors/Motors — Nidec showcases BLDC motors at AWE 2026; Embraco India factory ramps up

Nidec Global Appliance displayed Embraco compressors and Nidec motor technologies at AWE 2026. Construction of Embraco compressor factory in India advances toward ramp-up by end of 2026.

📍 India (for SEA supply) · Status: Under construction

Sourcing Implication

📜 Policy Focus

🇮🇩 Indonesia — MoT Reg 19/2026: New e-commerce rules, AI governance, TKDN enforcement

Indonesian Minister of Trade issued Regulation No. 19 of 2026, revoking MoT Reg 31/2023. Stricter compliance for e-commerce platforms and merchants: mandatory TKDN certification, product origin disclosure, AI governance rules, domestic product promotion algorithms. Five-tier sanctions framework. IMEI blocking for uncertified devices.

Effective: 8 June 2026
⚠️ Compliance Alert: E-commerce platforms must reassess seller licensing, cross-border trade, fee transparency. Non-compliance risks platform access blocking.

🇲🇾 Malaysia — SIRIM certification & LVG sales tax for electronics imports from May 2026

Effective 1 May 2026, Malaysia mandates SIRIM certification and local representative registration for electronic, electrical, and mechanical equipment. A 10% sales tax applies on cross-border direct shipments valued under MYR 500. eInvoicing compliance deadline extended to 31 Dec 2026 for certain taxpayers.

Effective: 1 May 2026
⚠️ Compliance Alert: International exporters must register local representatives; e-commerce platforms must ensure SIRIM compliance for listed electronics.

🇸🇬 Singapore — CSA & IMDA raise cybersecurity labelling for residential routers to Level 2 by end-2027

CSA and IMDA will raise mandatory cybersecurity requirements for residential routers from CLS Level 1 to Level 2 by end-2027. The upgrade follows discovery of 2,700 infected Singapore devices in a global botnet. IMDA also launched public consultation on eUICC technology.

Timeline: By end-2027

🇻🇳 Vietnam — Section 301 tariffs on Vietnamese electronics to increase to 25% from 24 July 2026

US Section 301 investigations target Vietnam, among other countries. If implemented, tariffs on Vietnamese electronics would rise from current 10% (Section 122) to 25%. Vietnam's reciprocal rate framework signed at 20%.

Effective: 24 July 2026 (proposed)
⚠️ Compliance Alert: Importers of Vietnamese-made electronics (servers, networking, consumer devices) face significant duty cost increases. Supply chain re-routing may accelerate.

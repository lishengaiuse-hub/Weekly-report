Motors & Actuators: Johnson Electric Opens Manufacturing Plant in Pillaipakkam

Johnson Electric International officially inaugurated its brand-new manufacturing plant in Pillaipakkam, Chennai, Tamil Nadu on 13 October 2026. The facility was established to scale up local volume manufacturing of high-reliability electric motors, motion subsystems, and electromechanical actuators for industrial machinery, automotive sub-assemblies, and consumer electronics platforms.

📍 Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India · Status: Operational

Localizing high-efficiency micro-motor and actuator production in the Chennai industrial corridor shortens supplier delivery windows for consumer durables and smart hardware OEMs operating in southern India.

13 Oct 2026 · Just-Auto · just-auto.com

Thermal Fluids: Chemours Partners with Navin Fluorine to Manufacture Opteon Immersion Coolant

The Chemours Company announced a formal agreement on 13–14 October 2026 with Navin Fluorine International Ltd. to manufacture its proprietary Opteon two-phase immersion cooling fluid within India. The agreement establishes domestic manufacturing of fluorochemical dielectric heat transfer liquids engineered to support the thermal dissipation loads of next-generation AI hardware, data centers, and high-performance electronics.

📍 Surat / Gujarat, India · Status: Commercialization Agreement Executed

Establishes indigenous manufacturing of advanced phase-change immersion cooling chemicals, reducing import dependency on specialized thermal management inputs as high-density hardware integration accelerates.

13 Oct 2026 · Contracting Business · contractingbusiness.com

Insulation Materials: Armacell Inaugurates New Pune Aerogel Insulation Facility

Flexible foam and equipment insulation manufacturer Armacell opened an aerogel insulation production plant in Pune, Maharashtra on 13 October 2026. Dedicated to producing the ArmaGel XG product family—including ArmaGel XGH (rated up to +650°C) and cryogenic ArmaGel XGC (-196°C to +250°C)—the new capital deployment doubles Armacell's operational aerogel capacity in India to serve HVAC, refrigeration, and specialized industrial equipment enclosures.

📍 Pune, Maharashtra, India · Status: Commissioned

Expands tier-2 supply availability of high-performance acoustic and thermal insulation blankets required by regional white-goods brands engineering higher energy efficiency ratings.

13 Oct 2026 · EQS News · eqs-news.com

Optoelectronics: Seoul Semiconductor in State Discussions for Large-Scale Indian Facility

South Korean LED manufacturer Seoul Semiconductor held formal discussions on 13 October 2026 with multiple Indian state governments—evaluating sites across Gujarat, Karnataka, and Tamil Nadu—to set up its first large-scale domestic manufacturing facility. The proposed facility is aimed at manufacturing optoelectronic components, advanced packaging, and LED sub-assemblies to service regional display, consumer lighting, and automotive electronics manufacturers.

📍 Gujarat / Karnataka / Tamil Nadu, India · Status: Site Evaluation & State Level Talks

Signals an impending domestic upstream source for LED emitters and packaging modules, supporting consumer electronics makers targeting higher domestic value addition under local sourcing mandates.

13 Oct 2026 · BIS Infotech · bisinfotech.com

BYD Electronics Initiates Construction on $183.7 Million Component and Battery Production Plant in Phu Tho

BYD Electronics initiated construction activities in mid-October 2026 on its $183.7 million manufacturing complex situated in northern Phu Tho province. The facility forms part of the group's phased industrial buildout in Vietnam, designated specifically for the high-volume fabrication of structural electronic components, mechanical assemblies, and consumer-grade rechargeable battery packs destined for international smart hardware brands.

The site expands the group's manufacturing presence in northern Vietnam beyond standard final assembly, adding precision parts tooling and dedicated pack integration capacity. Regulatory clearings and site preparation were finalized to maintain alignment with planned trial output schedules, feeding component inventories directly into regional finished-goods assembly networks.

Sourcing Implication

Expands tier-1 manufacturing depth for metal casings, precision structural components, and battery assemblies in northern Vietnam, offering an alternative integration route for brands hedging regional cross-border logistics costs.

14 Oct 2026 · Vietnam Investment Review · vir.com.vn

EMS / ODM BREAKING

Amber Enterprises Contracts Smartphone Production for Oppo, OnePlus, and Realme Beginning Q4 FY27

Indian contract manufacturing specialist Amber Enterprises finalized commercial terms on 17 October 2026 to commence assembly operations for BBK Group brands Oppo, OnePlus, and Realme. Production is scheduled to begin in the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2027 (March 2027), targeting an initial build volume of 8 million smartphone units in the first year of operation before scaling to 16 million units annually in year two.

The manufacturing mandate represents Amber's aggressive diversification beyond its traditional consumer durable and air-conditioning EMS foundation, leveraging its recent high-density interconnect (HDI) PCB acquisitions to deliver vertically integrated hardware assembly inside India.

Market Signal

Demonstrates structural transition among leading Chinese handset brands toward domestic tier-1 Indian EMS providers to fulfill local-market demand and build export-compliant production capacity under phased manufacturing targets.

17 Oct 2026 · Business Standard · business-standard.com

Adani Enterprises and Jabil Sign Strategic MoU for 50:50 Hardware Manufacturing Joint Venture

Adani Enterprises and global electronics manufacturing services provider Jabil Inc. executed a memorandum of understanding on 13 October 2026 to establish a 50:50 joint venture partnership in India. The collaborative venture is structured to build localized hardware manufacturing, precision engineering, and specialized assembly facilities targeting large-scale electronic and power infrastructure sectors.

The joint platform integrates Jabil's global electronics design, industrialization, and high-complexity manufacturing processes with Adani's domestic industrial infrastructure, positioning the operation to supply both domestic deployment programs and international original equipment manufacturers.

Strategic Read

Pairs top-tier global EMS manufacturing capabilities with local industrial land and energy assets, establishing high-volume capacity that bypasses import-tariff exposure across complex electronic systems.

13 Oct 2026 · Jabil Newsroom · jabil.com

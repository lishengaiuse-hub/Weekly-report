Global Industry Shifts — SEA Impact

MLCC Price Surge Reshapes Component Sourcing

Murata raised high-capacitance MLCC prices 10–15% effective July 1, 2026. Samsung Electro-Mechanics followed with a 30% hike starting August 1, and Taiyo Yuden is reportedly planning a September 1 increase. The squeeze is concentrated in high-capacitance, large-case grades feeding AI server power delivery and automotive lines.

→ Downstream SEA impact: Smartphone and appliance BOM costs in Vietnam, Thailand, and Malaysia face upward pressure; procurement teams should audit BOMs by case size and capacitance grade.
Source: TrendForce · jcwyic.com · LinkedIn

US Tariff Reset on Seven ASEAN Nations

The United States introduced a new round of tariffs on seven ASEAN countries, replacing the temporary 10% levy that expired July 24, 2026. Cambodia, Indonesia, and Malaysia face 10%; the Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, and Vietnam face 12.5%. Vietnam's reciprocal rate is at 20% under a framework agreement; Malaysia's at 19%.

→ Downstream SEA impact: Export-oriented electronics manufacturers in Vietnam and Thailand must factor higher US-bound costs; Malaysia's 19% reciprocal rate offers marginal advantage.
Source: Seasia Stats · Dimerco · tariffstool.com

SEA Smartphone ASP Hits Record High

Southeast Asia's smartphone market declined 9% YoY in Q1 2026 with shipments at 21.6 million units. ASP reached a record $349, up 19% YoY, driven by memory cost inflation resetting device pricing. Samsung is the only top-5 brand growing in the region.

→ Downstream SEA impact: Vendor landscape undergoing structural shift toward premium; mid-range Chinese OEMs face margin compression.
Source: Omdia · Android Headlines · Light Reading

China+1 Strategy Solidifies ASEAN as Production Base

Southeast Asia electronics manufacturing services market is estimated at $28.91 billion in 2026, growing at 10.01% CAGR through 2031. Expanding FDI, government incentives, and the China+1 diversification push are elevating ASEAN as a preferred production base. Vietnam and Indonesia are key alternatives.

→ Downstream SEA impact: EMS capacity in Vietnam, Thailand, and Malaysia continues to scale; component localization deepening.
Source: Mordor Intelligence · A2 Global Electronics

Passive Components

MLCC Price Hikes Across the Board

Samsung Electro-Mechanics lifted MLCC prices 30% starting August 1, 2026. The company secured AI server MLCC orders worth KRW 453.99 billion and KRW 295.12 billion in June and July. 📍 Global · Status: Acquisition completed

Refrigeration compressor supply consolidates; SEA appliance makers gain access to integrated motor-compressor solutions.

Policy Focus — SEA Regulatory Watch

🇮🇩 Indonesia — Permendag 19/2026 E-Commerce Overhaul

Indonesia's new e-commerce regulation widens categories of businesses subject to PMSE rules, tightens conditions for cross-border sellers, mandates algorithmic changes for domestic product promotion, and introduces the country's first AI governance rules in a commercial context. Platforms are directly responsible for seller compliance. A five-tier sanctions framework backs the regulation.

Status: In effect · Compliance required for foreign sellers
⚠ Foreign merchants face significantly higher registration and disclosure requirements.

🇮🇩 Indonesia — Revamped TKDN Rules

New local content regulation introduces a filtering mechanism for goods not eligible for certification. Only goods produced from industrial activities in Indonesia per KBLI can be certified. TKDN calculation cannot apply to goods whose entire raw materials are imported.

Status: In effect
⚠ Import-dependent electronics face TKDN certification hurdles.

🇲🇾 Malaysia — MCMC (Amendment) Bill 2026 Passed

The Dewan Rakyat passed the MCMC (Amendment) Bill 2026 on July 15 to ensure the Commission remains sustainable in regulating and developing Malaysia's communications and multimedia industry. Digital service tax at 8% applies; mandatory registration if digital service revenue exceeds RM500,000 annually.

Status: Passed July 15, 2026

🇸🇬 Singapore — Router Cybersecurity Level Raised

CSA and IMDA will raise mandatory cybersecurity certification for residential routers from CLS Level 1 to Level 2 by end 2027. Level 2 requires secure communications, secure storage of sensitive data, and robust authentication mechanisms.

Status: Announced March 2026 · Effective end 2027
⚠ Router manufacturers must redesign for Level 2 compliance.

🇻🇳 🇹🇭 🇵🇭 — US Tariff Framework Agreements

Vietnam's reciprocal rate at 20% with potential 0% duty on some Annex III articles. Malaysia's rate at 19% with similar Annex III provisions. Philippines benefits from zero tariffs under ATIGA for ASEAN-origin goods. New US tariffs: Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam at 12.5%; Cambodia, Indonesia, Malaysia at 10%.

Status: Effective from July 24, 2026
⚠ Exporters to US must re-evaluate landed cost models.

Indonesia's new local content regulation introduces a filtering mechanism for goods not eligible for certification. Only goods produced from industrial activities in Indonesia per KBLI can be certified. TKDN calculation cannot apply to goods whose entire raw materials are imported.

Philippines Benefits from ATIGA Zero Tariffs

The Philippines has eliminated tariffs on approximately 99% of ASEAN-origin goods under the ASEAN Trade in Goods Agreement (ATIGA). This provides zero or near-zero tariff rates on goods originating from any of the 10 ASEAN member states.
